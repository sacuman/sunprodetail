Our Story

PROFESSIONAL DETAILING BUILT ON REAL EXPERIENCE

SUN PRO is built on hands-on vehicle preparation and detailing experience. We assess each car properly, recommend only the work it needs, and focus on a finish that makes a visible difference.

Our work is supported by senior technical guidance with more than 25 years of experience, together with hands-on detailing experience dating back to 2012. Working within the Wangara Motors environment also gives us regular experience across many vehicle types and paint conditions.

That practical background shapes a simple approach: inspect first, explain the options clearly, agree on the result and price, then allow enough booked time to do the job properly.
